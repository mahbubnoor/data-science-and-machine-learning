What makes a good project

1

A clear question

One sentence: what you predict or discover, and for whom it would be useful.

2

Data you can get

Enough rows (a few hundred at least), legally and ethically obtained, usable offline.

3

An honest measure

A metric that matches the cost of mistakes, and a baseline to beat.

Pitfalls to avoid

1

Too big

One clear question beats five vague ones. Shrink the scope early.

2

No baseline

A score means nothing without a simple guess to compare with.

3

Peeking at the test set

Choose everything with cross-validation; test once, at the end.
