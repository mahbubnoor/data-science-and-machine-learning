How to use

Slides: →/Space next step, ← back, F full screen, a number and Enter to jump, S slide list. The bar on top switches sessions ([ ]) and opens the lab (L). Press ? for all keys.

Labs: each lab runs Python ideas in the browser and checks every number against real Python and scikit-learn. Its bar goes back to the slides, or to the previous or next lab. Then write the same code in Jupyter or Colab.

Offline: everything is in this folder: slides, videos, labs, data and fonts. Copy it to a lab PC and open index.html in any browser, no internet needed.

Instructor: press N on any slide to show or hide the speaker notes under the slide (your choice is remembered). PPTX downloads the PowerPoint deck with notes. The student edition has neither.
